Autoterm Heater Module

The ConnectedVan Autoterm Heater module is a custom, ESP32-C6 based hardware bridge designed to safely integrate Autoterm diesel heaters into your smart van ecosystem.

By utilizing the proprietary Autoterm serial protocol, this module grants Home Assistant full control over your heating system while maintaining complete electrical isolation between the vehicle's high-power 12V/24V chassis and the microcontroller's sensitive 3.3V logic.

Key Features​

  • Galvanic Isolation: Digital isolators (ADUM1201) protect the ESP32 from voltage spikes on the heater bus.
  • Passthrough Architecture: Retain your original Autoterm wall controller as a physical fallback.
  • Smart Power Routing: Power the board directly from the heater harness or via an external battery.
  • Controller Re-initialization: An onboard Solid State Relay (SSR) allows the ESP32 to digitally cut power to the original controller, forcing it to resync if the microcontroller restarts.

Technical Specifications​

Power Configurations & Wiring​

The board is highly flexible and can adapt to different van architectures. Power routing is dictated by the two jumpers located at J2 (bottom right of the board).

Power from Heater (Default)Power from External Source
J2 configured to the H positionJ2 configured to the E position

Variant 1: Power from Heater (Default)​

This is the simplest, plug-and-play setup for most users. The board draws its logic power directly from the heater's wiring harness, meaning the smart system is online and functioning whenever the heater has power.

  1. Jumper Configuration: Ensure both jumpers on J2 are set to the H (Heater) position.
  2. Wiring: Plug the cable coming from your Autoterm heater directly into the JST HEATER port.

Variant 2: External Power Source​

Use this variant if your heater is powered from a different battery bank than your smart system. Because the board is galvanically isolated, cross-battery setups are completely safe. This variant is also ideal if you want the ability to shut off your entire smart system from a single centralized place while leaving the heater untouched.

  1. Jumper Configuration: Move both jumpers on J2 to the E (External) position.
  2. Wiring: Connect your smart system's 12V/24V power supply to the V_IN and GND pins on the lower-left terminal.

Optional Add-ons (For Both Variants)​

Regardless of how you power the board, you can utilize the following independent features to enhance your setup:

  • Original Controller: Connect your physical Autoterm control panel to the CONTROLLER JST port. The ESP32 can electronically cut power to this controller. This is primarily required to force the original controller to re-initialize if the ESP32 restarts, ensuring the UART communication between all devices remains perfectly synchronized.
  • Custom Button & LED: You can connect an external button, an external LED, or a combination of both (like a 5V illuminated push-button). Connect a momentary switch between BTN and +5V. Connect an indicator LED between LED and +5V (ensure you use a proper resistor for your specific LED, as the output is 5V). Because these have no default function, they are fantastic for triggering custom Home Assistant routines.
  • External Triggers (IN_1 / IN_2): Connect external voltage sources to these pins (sharing the terminal GND). These are intended for smart system triggers and, in future firmware updates, for waking the ESP32 up from deep sleep.

Firmware Flashing & Quick Start​

To get your board online and communicating with your van's network, connect the module to your computer via USB-C and use the web flasher below.


Network Setup & Home Assistant Pairing​

Once you have successfully flashed the firmware to the board via USB, follow these steps to get it onto your van's network:

  1. Connect to the Captive Portal:
    • Power on the module.
    • Open your phone or laptop's Wi-Fi settings and look for a network named CVan Autoterm Heater.
    • Connect to this network. A captive portal page should automatically pop up. (If it doesn't, navigate to http://192.168.4.1 in your browser).
  2. Enter Wi-Fi Credentials:
    • Select your van's Wi-Fi network from the list and enter the password. Click Save.
    • The board will reboot and connect to your local network.
  3. Add to Home Assistant:
    • Open your Home Assistant dashboard and navigate to Settings > Devices & Services.
    • Home Assistant should automatically discover the new ESPHome device. Click Configure.
    • If it is not automatically discovered, click Add Integration, search for "ESPHome", and enter the IP address of the board.
    • The Autoterm Heater, along with the optional Button, LED, and Input sensors, will now be available to use in your dashboards and automations!
